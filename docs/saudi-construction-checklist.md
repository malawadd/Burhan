# Saudi construction lifecycle evidence checklist

Generated from the research catalog on 2026-10-20. 89 inspection families across 16 stages.

Scope: **both low-rise residential and commercial buildings**, with separate applicability notes in every row. These are review tasks, not claims of implemented detection or a legally exhaustive checklist. Exact code applicability and acceptance rules require controlled documents and the project authority. All entries are initially **unassessed**. `video_visual` identifies a possible visible observation; acceptance still needs the evidence listed.

The verified SBC1101-1102 description concerns qualifying one-/two-family dwellings no more than three stories. A low-rise apartment or mixed-use building does not automatically qualify. Commercial occupancy and systems determine its SBC201/discipline route. Energy, fire and accessibility criteria can differ; no unverified numerical thresholds are supplied. See the [both-scope comparison and source limitations](saudi-construction-inspection-study.md#low-rise-residential-and-commercial-scope).
