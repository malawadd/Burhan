## Minimal proposed experiment for a later decision

Start with **one crack channel** and unchanged384 serving topology. A small standalone trainer can reuse the reviewed model constructor/strict TUT loading without author Visdom/config side effects or unavailable local loss/tool files. Freeze encoder initially and train the learned bottleneck/adapters/ECA/upsampling/CABlock/Fuse/final decoder; preserve frozen BN and explicitly disabled stochastic DropPath during this low-data continuation. Gradients work in eval mode. The existing export's DropPath identity substitution is inference-only provenance, not an undisclosed copy of author training behavior.

Use actual LCSD crack masks, DACL Crack/ACrack polygon unions and target-negative TRAIN rehearsal with matched image/mask geometry. Keep source-image groups together and never add validation/heldout photos. Native positive-centered TRAIN crops can preserve fine detail; retain all visible actual labels, include unbiased/background crops, and audit empty/vanished targets. Use logits BCE plus a soft overlap term, not rectangle filling; fix sampling, weights, steps/checkpoints, transformations and full calibration rule **before** fitting. A proposed bound is one fixed300-step continuation with100/200/300 checkpoints, batch1, after a small full-shape backward/memory probe; this is a reviewable workload bound, not a measured duration or permission to launch. Do not select using LCSD TRAIN scores or future video outcomes.

Spall is optional **separate work**, not a free output: changing final `5→1` to `5→2` needs a declared initialization/new class mapping, partial-label loss and two-channel adapter/export/parity fixtures. On LCSD, set spall validity **zero everywhere**, not targetzero with full loss. On DACL, rasterize independent Crack/ACrack and Spalling unions; respect overlaps and known annotation scope. Normalize masked loss by valid pixels/channels, exclude unknown regions from BCE and overlap terms, and avoid empty-valid-channel divisions. A crack-only first experiment has fewer new assumptions and reuses serving unchanged.
