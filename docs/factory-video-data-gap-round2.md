# Factory video data gap: bounded source check

Checked 20 October 2026, using primary dataset/project pages and papers. This was a bounded search, not an exhaustive claim that suitable data cannot exist. No video archive was downloaded, no model ran, and no screenshot, image slideshow or generated clip was substituted for captured factory video.

**No verified source found in this check supplies an immediately usable, publicly licensed, genuine commutator-production video with timestamped spatial defect labels matching the current KolektorSDD model.** The existing still-image accuracy study and replay throughput test must remain separately labelled. The closest industrial video candidates have material annotation, release, licensing or domain limitations.

| Primary source | What is actually described / available | Labels and license verification | Suitability decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| [AeBAD / official MMR repository](https://github.com/zhangzilongc/MMR), [author paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2304.02216v2) | Aero-engine blade inspection. AeBAD-V consists of real sequential video observations released as consecutive frames; the paper notes real engine blades **or 1:1 replicas**. Three test sequences have varying views/lighting. Official Drive archive landing page resolves; archive contents and original timebase were not downloaded/verified here. | Authors explicitly release data under **CC BY 4.0**, separately from Apache code. Official README says AeBAD-V evaluates **sample-level only**, with pixel-level metric unavailable/zero. | Best lead for a separately trained blade-video anomaly experiment, but lacks verified spatial defect annotations/timebase for the requested localization acceptance. Blade inspection is different from commutator surface inspection. Cannot claim existing model works on it. |
| [Intel Robotic Welding Multimodal Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/IntelLabs/Intel_Robotic_Welding_Multimodal_Dataset/blob/main/README.md), [author paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.02290v1) | Genuine automotive-floor robotic welding, 4,040 weld samples, camera video plus audio/sensors/post-weld images. Video nominally 30 FPS AVI; paper documents timing/alignment variability. These are captured physical welds, with defects deliberately induced by robot settings rather than computer-rendered images. | Explicit **Intel Research Use License**, noncommercial/research only, access request required; anonymous access requests rejected. The paper states **one label per weld with no indication when the defect occurs**. No verified per-frame spatial defect ground truth. | Useful for a different multimodal welding task, not a public drop-in localized commutator video benchmark. No access request or download made. |
| [IPAD official project](https://ljf1113.github.io/IPAD_VAD/), [official repository](https://github.com/LJF1113/IPAD) | Six hours across 16 industrial devices, expressly a **mixture of synthetic and real footage**. Dataset Drive landing page resolves; real-only file provenance and archive contents were not inspected. Targets process periodicity/anomalies rather than the current product surface task. | Dataset link exists, but a clear reusable **dataset** license was not verified in the reviewed material. Website footer licenses the **website** CC BY-SA; that must not be assumed to license videos. Spatial defect masks/timebase matching our task were not verified. | Do not ingest indiscriminately or describe as all-real factory surface evidence. Not used. |
| [MMVIAD official repository](https://github.com/Georgekeepmoving/MMVIAD), [author paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.10833v1) | Two-second camera trajectories look directly relevant in search summaries. The actual paper explicitly says **controllable rendering**, including aligned red-marked/unmarked anomalous object renders. Repository release plan still says data/model links will be added. | Visible-time labels are described, but genuine captured footage and a usable released dataset license are not established. | **Exclude:** rendered data does not meet the user's real-video requirement. A promising title is not proof of real footage or actual availability. |
| [CCTV-Pipe official dataset page](https://videopipe.github.io/cctvpipe/index.html), [official code/download instructions](https://github.com/Richard-61/VideoPipe) | Real urban pipe inspection, 575 videos / 87 hours, 16 defect categories. Engineers annotate a preferred single defect frame/moment in untrimmed video. | Academic copyright form/competition terms required; public unrestricted redistribution license not verified. Temporal moments are not full spatial masks. | Real defect video exists in an adjacent inspection field, but pipes are outside the factory model's domain; no universal detector support implied. Not acquired. |

The original [KolektorSDD2 author release](https://www.vicos.si/resources/kolektorsdd2/) supplies industrial **images**, and the acquired ZIP is an image/mask dataset. Its larger training set can improve learning without creating a genuine source video. Similarly, [MVTec AD](https://www.mvtec.com/research-teaching/datasets/mvtec-ad) describes images, not a production video timebase. A video encoded by cycling these stills would be a clearly labelled load replay, not new video-localization evidence.

For the current product, valid future video acceptance needs actual captured product passages, original timestamps/FPS/exposure, physical-item or cycle identities, positive and normal passages, and independent defect locations in visible frames. Split by physical item/session before training or calibration. Video-frame count cannot be treated as an equal number of independent defect examples. The present work does not silently replace that missing evidence with an unrelated dataset or source-authored overlay.
